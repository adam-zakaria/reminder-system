# 🌟 **User Manual for AI-Caring Website** 🌟

Welcome to the **AI-Caring Platform**! This tool helps you create smart reminders for your daily routines. Follow these simple steps to get started:

---

## 🚪 **Step 1: Register**
1. 🌐 Visit [**AI-Caring Platform**](https://gateway.parcs.northeastern.edu/ai-caring/).
2. 🔘 Click **Register** and fill in:
   - **Name**
   - **Email**
   - **Password**
3. 📩 Submit the form and get started.

---

## 🔑 **Step 2: Log in**
1. Go to [**AI-Caring Platform**](https://gateway.parcs.northeastern.edu/ai-caring/).
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. 🎉 Click **Login** to access your dashboard.

---

## 🤖 **Step 3: Chat with the Bot**
- Navigate to the chatbot.
- Use natural language to create reminders.
- Be creative and explore what the bot can do!

---

### ✨ **Examples**:
- 🧹 *"Remind me to clean the kitchen after breakfast."*
- 🔥 *"If the stove is on and I’m not cooking, remind me to turn it off."*
- 💊 *"Remind me to take my medicine an hour after lunch."*
- 📺 *"Help me stop watching TV after 3 hours."*

---
